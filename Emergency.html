Essential First Aid in an Emergency

Red Cross Certified

Heart Attack

Lay the patient flat immediately and check breathing. Place both hands in the centre of the chest and compress at 100–120 times per minute (CPR). Call 108 immediately.

Stroke

Face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech — these are warning signs. Do not waste a single minute. Take the patient immediately to the AIIMS or Apollo emergency department.

Severe Bleeding

Apply firm pressure to the wound with a clean cloth or bandage. Keep the injured limb elevated above heart level until help arrives.

# SwastikCare 2.0 🏥

> **India's Leading Integrated Digital Healthcare Ecosystem**  
> Direct real-time connectivity with India's top 5 super-specialty hospitals: **AIIMS New Delhi**, **Indraprastha Apollo**, **Max Super Speciality**, **Fortis Memorial (FMRI)**, and **Medanta - The Medicity**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Direct Hospital Network**: Live vacant general bed and ICU telemetry across 5,360+ beds in premier Delhi-NCR medical centres.
- **25 Eminent Senior Specialists**: 5 dedicated department heads per hospital conducting daily OPD consultations.
- **OPD & Telehealth Booking**: 4-step streamlined booking for in-person hospital visits or HD telehealth video consultations.
- **ABHA (ABDM) Digital Health Locker**: Encrypted, verified Electronic Health Records (EHR), lab diagnostic reports, and digital prescriptions.
- **Live Vitals & Biometrics Telemetry**: Real-time tracking of Heart Rate, Blood Pressure, SpO2, Blood Glucose, Body Temperature, and BMI.
- **MoveOn Physical Rehabilitation**: Personalized milestone roadmap for post-operative recovery, ACL ligament surgery rehab, and daily exercise streaks.
- **24/7 Emergency SOS 108 Dispatch**: 1-click GPS-synchronized ambulance dispatch and emergency trauma bay coordination.
- **Hospital Staff & Super Admin Portals**: Dedicated portals for bed occupancy management, patient queues, and nationwide health governance.
- **Fully Responsive & Accessible**: Designed to fit seamlessly across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop workstations.

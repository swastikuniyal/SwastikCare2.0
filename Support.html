Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I reschedule my appointment? Navigate to the 'Book Doctor' page, pick your preferred doctor and date — your updated slot and token will be generated instantly.
Are my health records secure? Yes, all medical records and diagnostic files comply with the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) standards, encrypted with role-based access.
What should I do in a critical emergency? Press the red 'Emergency 108' button in the navigation bar or dial 108 immediately to dispatch the nearest emergency ambulance.
